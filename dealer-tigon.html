Authorized Low Speed Vehicle Dealer — Why It Matters

Choosing an authorized low speed vehicle dealer is one of the most important decisions you'll make when buying an LSV. An authorized low speed vehicle dealer has a direct relationship with the manufacturers it represents, which means factory-backed training, genuine parts, and warranty service you can rely on. Independent resellers may advertise a low price, but they often cannot provide manufacturer support, correct documentation, or the parts and service you'll need down the road. As an authorized low speed vehicle dealer, Tigon sells vehicles that meet FMVSS No. 500, provides the VIN and Manufacturer's Certificate of Origin required to title and register your LSV, and backs every sale with local service across 11 states. When you buy from an authorized dealer, you get a properly documented, street-legal vehicle and a partner for the life of your LSV.

Why Choose a Certified LSV Dealer?

What makes an LSV dealer certified?

A certified low speed vehicle dealer is an authorized retailer that sells LSVs built to federal safety standards and supports them with proper titling, service, and warranty coverage. Rather than a single national license, 'certified' reflects a dealer's authorization to sell specific LSV brands, its compliance with state dealer-licensing and registration rules, and its commitment to delivering vehicles that meet Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard No. 500. Tigon operates as an authorized, certified low speed vehicle dealer across 11 states.

What certifications should a low speed vehicle dealer have?

Look for a low speed vehicle dealer that is an authorized seller for the brands it carries, holds any dealer license your state requires, and sells vehicles that fully comply with FMVSS No. 500 — the federal standard defining a low speed vehicle. A reputable dealer will also provide a Manufacturer's Certificate of Origin and a VIN so the LSV can be titled and registered. Tigon meets these standards and helps buyers complete registration in their state.

Is Tigon a DOT-approved LSV dealer?

'DOT approval' for low speed vehicles applies to the vehicles themselves, not to dealers: the U.S. Department of Transportation, through NHTSA, sets Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard No. 500 that every LSV must meet. Tigon sells low speed vehicles manufactured to comply with FMVSS No. 500, and as an authorized dealer ensures each vehicle is properly equipped and documented for legal road use. Your state DMV handles final titling and registration.

Why buy from a certified low speed vehicle dealer instead of an online reseller?

Buying from a certified low speed vehicle dealer means your LSV arrives street-legal, correctly titled, and safety-equipped to FMVSS No. 500 — not a golf cart marketed as road-ready. A local certified dealer provides in-person inspection, genuine parts, warranty service, and guidance on your state's registration and insurance rules. Unverified online resellers often cannot provide the documentation you need to register the vehicle, which can turn a low price into an expensive problem.
