Low Speed Vehicle Dealership FAQ

What is a low speed vehicle dealership?

A low speed vehicle dealership is a certified retailer that sells and services street-legal low speed vehicles (LSVs) — electric vehicles capped at 20–25 mph and approved for roads posted at 35 mph or less. Unlike a general golf cart shop, a low speed vehicle dealership offers vehicles built to federal safety standards with headlights, turn signals, mirrors, seat belts, and a VIN, so they can be registered and driven legally on public streets. LSVDealer.com is a national directory connecting buyers with certified low speed vehicle dealerships across the United States.

What should I look for in a certified LSV dealer?

When choosing a certified LSV dealer, look for a low speed vehicle dealership that sells street-legal vehicles meeting federal safety standards, offers on-site service and parts, and clearly explains your state's registration and insurance requirements. A good low speed vehicle dealership will help you match the right vehicle — personal transport, utility, or passenger — to how you plan to use it, provide a written warranty, and support you after the sale.
