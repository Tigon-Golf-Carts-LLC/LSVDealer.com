How to Buy a Low Speed Vehicle

Ready to buy a low speed vehicle? The process is straightforward:

  • 1. Choose a model. Decide how you will use your LSV and pick the body style, seating, and battery range that fit.
  • 2. Find a certified dealer. Use our directory to buy an LSV from an authorized dealer near you.
  • 3. Confirm DOT/FMVSS compliance. Every LSV you buy should meet FMVSS No. 500 so it is street legal and titleable.
  • 4. Arrange payment or financing. Ask your dealer about available payment and financing options.
  • 5. Register the vehicle. Title, register, and insure your LSV through your state DMV — your dealer will guide you.

Follow those steps and you can buy a low speed vehicle with confidence, knowing the LSV you buy is street legal, correctly documented, and supported close to home.
