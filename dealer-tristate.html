Does TriState Golf Cars sell street-legal golf carts?

Yes. TriState Golf Cars sells street-legal low speed vehicles (LSVs) and electric golf carts equipped with headlights, turn signals, mirrors, seat belts, and a windshield for legal use on roads posted at 35 mph or less.

Low speed vehicles (LSVs) are street legal on most roads posted at 35 mph or less when equipped with the required safety features. TriState Golf Cars can help you find a street-legal golf cart that meets your local requirements.
